# Roblox Game Success 2024–2026: Comprehensive Research Report

Research conducted 2026-09-22. Sources marked `index` (web index) or `verified live` (read in live browser).
Readers: all CCU figures are concurrent-player peaks; "visits" are lifetime cumulative joins (they never decay).

---

## Summary

- **2024** belonged to evergreen RP/social giants (Brookhaven, Adopt Me) and the anime action-RPG Blox Fruits, plus fashion-phenomenon Dress to Impress. Genres: roleplay, simulators, obby, horror.
- **2025** was the year of the idle/cozy simulator and the "brainrot" meme economy: Grow a Garden peaked at **22.4M CCU** and Steal a Brainrot at **25.8M CCU** — both all-time records for any gaming platform. Survival (99 Nights in the Forest, 14.2M) and horror (Forsaken, Dandy's World) also broke out.
- **2026** is a comedown: platform DAUs fell from the 152M peak (Q3 2025) to ~123M (Q2 2026); Grow a Garden collapsed to ~50K CCU; Roblox's discovery algorithm pivoted hard to long-term retention at the expense of short-term monetization, causing a bookings shortfall, a $9B single-day value loss and a ~70% share-price drawdown. Evergreens (Brookhaven, Blox Fruits, Adopt Me) reclaimed the top of live charts.
- The money machine: creators keep **70%** of in-experience Robux spend; DevEx cash-out is **$0.0038/Robux** (standard, raised Sep 2025) and **$0.0054** for verified US 18+ spend (since Jun 2026). Roblox paid **$1.5B** to creators in 2025; the median DevEx creator made ~$1,500/yr while the top 1,000 averaged ~$1.3M.
- Solo/small-team development remains the norm for hits: Brookhaven was solo-built (sold for an estimated $100M+), Grow a Garden was prototyped in days by a 16-year-old, MM2 is essentially solo.

**Fall-off patterns:**
1. **Content drought / dev abandonment** — Rainbow Friends (radio silence), Meep City. The #1 killer: Roblox's discovery algorithm punishes stagnant retention signals, so dead games vanish from recommendations.
2. **Viral novelty with no systems depth** — Dead Rails, brainrot clones; 90%+ drawdowns within months.
3. **Monetization backlash** — DTI (FOMO/p2w), Fisch under DoBig, Grow a Garden (Robux-skip-everything complaints).
4. **Creator churn** — Jandel leaving Grow a Garden's day-to-day; players follow personalities ("admin abuse" live events were appointment viewing).
5. **Platform rule changes** — Be Dead Forever Simulator (age ratings).
6. **Platform-level 2026 slump** — DAUs 152M → 123M; age-verification friction (only ~51% of users verified by Q1 2026), Russia ban (Dec 2025), and no 2025-scale viral replacement — `index`, https://www.ainvest.com/news/roblox-blames-quality-monetization-push-slumping-player-numbers-spending-2608/.

**Counter-pattern — comebacks:** Blox Fruits and Fisch both posted major player revivals after big updates, showing dormant audiences return when live-ops resume (`index`, https://www.youtube.com/shorts/BUAqH-n6IUs).

## Q4 — How Roblox discovery works (2025–2026)

### The official mechanics (Roblox creator docs, current) — `index`, https://github.com/roblox/creator-docs/blob/HEAD/content/en-us/discovery.md

"Recommended for You" ranks in two stages:
1. **Retrieval** — candidate pool from signals: engagement, retention, monetization, connection (friend) plays, similar experiences, variety.
2. **Ranking** — six signals:
   - **Qualified play-through rate (qPTR)** — engaging plays ÷ impressions
   - **7-day playtime per user**
   - **7-day play days per user** (repeat visits)
   - **7-day spend days per user**
   - **7-day Robux spent per user**
   - **7-day intentional co-play days per user** (joins via friends/invites, not matchmaking)

Monetization explicitly feeds discovery: games that get players spending across multiple days rank higher (`index`, https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2025-roblox-report). The system handles **~10.5B daily impressions** (`index`, ainvest.com).

### 2025: refinement era
- Roblox's stated 2025 focus: revamped home screen, "organic recommendations," blended search, **reducing low-quality content** (`index`, https://passionfru.it/roblox-promises-161535/).
- Friend-referral program drove **+90% co-play sessions**.

### 2026: the retention pivot (major)
- Roblox retuned recommendations to **optimize for long-term retention** (testing **28-day retention windows**) instead of short-term monetization (`index`, https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/2932408/rblxs-discovery-shift-targets-retention-can-monetization-hold-up).
- CEO Baszucki (Q2 2026 earnings): "focus our discovery algorithms directly on measured long-term retention… lower frequency of impressions with games that emphasize short-term monetization rather than long-term retention." CFO: the algorithm now gives "more impressions for highly retentive games at the expense of near-term monetization" — this caused the **bookings shortfall in under-13 cohorts** (`index`, https://massivelyop.com/2026/08/06/roblox-stonks-plummeted-as-its-shift-toward-long-term-retention-sees-kids-spending-less-money/; https://kotaku.com/roblox-tanked-9-billion-in-a-single-day-after-the-company-admitted-it-was-directing-fewer-kids-toward-viral-games-that-emphasize-short-term-monetization-2000722002).
- Consequence: **$9B single-day market value loss** (Jul 2026), ~**70% share-price drawdown** from peak, Q3 2026 bookings guided **down 14–18% YoY**.
- Early evidence it works as intended: in Q1 2026, **games outside the top 10 drove 65% of spending growth** — discovery breadth improved (`index`, zacks.com).
- Age-check data is being wired into targeting (recommendations by age cohort/region).
- Platform moves: **Roblox Build** (make a game from a mobile prompt via agentic AI), **standalone apps** (publish Roblox games off-platform), **Roblox Wallet + Card** for creators — announced Sep 2026 (`index`, https://www.trefis.com/stock/rblx/articles/615344/why-did-roblox-stock-surge-past-the-target-an-analyst-just-raised/2026-09-15).

**What gets promoted now:** high qPTR (click → actually plays), multi-day repeat play, long sessions, friend co-play, and (still) repeat spending — but 2026 weights durable retention above monetization spikes. Short-burst viral monetization games get fewer impressions than before.

## Q5 — Monetization: how top games earn

### Revenue streams
1. **Game passes** (one-time): Brookhaven Premium 275 R$, PS99 VIP 400 R$, Blox Fruits 2x Mastery etc. Creator keeps **~70%** of in-experience spend after platform fee.
2. **Developer products** (repeatable consumables): PS99 egg packs (100 Exclusive Eggs 2,370 R$), Blox Fruits fruits (50–15,000 R$), Grow a Garden fertilizers/seeds.
3. **Season/battle passes**: Grow a Garden Season Pass Premium **749 R$**; level skip 49 R$; full skip 1,699 R$ (`index`, https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/grow-garden-season-3-pass-guide-premium-price-rewards).
4. **Private servers**: recurring monthly Robux fees (Brookhaven sells server perks).
5. **Engagement payouts → Creator Rewards**: Premium Payouts (engagement-based, since 2020) were **deprecated 24 Jul 2025** and replaced by **Creator Rewards** (`index`, https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/monetization/engagement-based-payouts.md; https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/06/24/roblox-creator-rewards-program-robux-for-engagement-retention/):
   - **Daily Engagement Reward**: 5 Robux each time an "Active Spender" ($9.99+ spent in past 60 days) spends 10+ minutes in your experience — only their first 3 experiences/day count.
   - **Audience Expansion Reward**: 35% of the first $100 spent by new users or users inactive 60+ days who arrive via the creator.
6. **Immersive ads** (image/portal ads revenue share), **rewarded video ads** (Google partnership, 2025), **IP licensing marketplace**, IRL merch (DTI via Claire's in 866 stores).

### The money math
- **DevEx**: $0.0038 per earned Robux (raised from $0.0035 on 5 Sep 2025); **$0.0054** for verified US 18+ spend (since 8 Jun 2026, ~42% premium); min cash-out 30,000 R$ (~$114) — `index`, https://www.gemlist.io/blog/does-roblox-devex-actually-pay; https://www.techtimes.com/articles/326712/20260904/roblox-creator-economy-paid-444m-non-tech-communities-bookings-now-falling.htm.
- Roblox paid **$1.5B** to creators in 2025 (up from $923M in 2024). Median DevEx participant: ~**$1,500/year**; top 1,000 averaged **~$1.3M** each in 2025 (+50% YoY); top 100 ~$6M each (`index`, sqmagazine.co.uk/roblox-statistics; techtimes.com).
- Whale dynamics: top 5% of payers spend **>30x** the bottom 50% (was ~20x in 2023); whales >$20/day; median payer <$1 (`index`, gameanalytics.com 2025 report).

## Q6 — Player demographics and session behavior

### Demographics
- **Age**: 58% of DAUs are now **over 13** (2025). Split: 7–12: 34%, 13–17: 40%, 18–24: 19%, 25+: 9% (`index`, https://playblox-tools.com/statistics/player-demographics-2025/). Among age-checked users (Q1 2026): 35% <13, 38% 13–17, 27% 18+; the **18+ cohort is ~45%+ of DAUs** and monetizes **>50% higher** than younger users; 18–34 grew >50% YoY (`index`, https://sqmagazine.co.uk/roblox-statistics/; https://world.infonasional.com/roblox-stock-growth-adult-users).
- **Gender**: 51% male, 44–46% female, ~3–5% non-binary/other — near parity, unusual for gaming (`index`, analyticsinsight.net; playblox-tools.com).
- **Geography**: US 26%, Brazil 18%, Philippines 10%, UK 8%, India 6%, Mexico 5%, Germany 4%, rest 23%. APAC fastest-growing (India +57%, Japan +56% in 2025) (`index`, playblox-tools.com; yeahthatcourtney.com).
- **Kids penetration**: ~50% of US children under 16 play monthly (`index`, https://www.analyticsinsight.net/gaming/top-statistics-on-roblox-to-know).
- **Kids' genre tastes** (Australia, Roy Morgan Dec 2025): Adventure > Action > Simulation; boys 2x+ into Fighting/Shooters/Action; girls prefer Fashion/Music/Party (`index`, https://roymorgan-cms-prod.s3.ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/03043203/10136-YAS-Roblox-for-Young-Australians-Survey-December-2025-002.pdf).

### Session behavior (GameAnalytics 2025 & 2026 benchmark reports — `index`, https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2025-roblox-report; https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)
- **Session length**: median **7.28 min**; P75 14.85 min; P90 26.3 min; P95 37.2 min; P99 74.8 min. (Per-experience dashboards typically show 10–14 min.)
- **Daily playtime**: median 22.4 min; P75 58.7 min; P90 123.9 min (~2h); P95 186.5 min; P99 ~6.7h. Roblox's own figure: average user ~**2.7 hours/day** platform-wide.
- **Frequency**: half of players log in **multiple times per day**; most-active core users hit **up to 8 sessions/day**; average ~2.3 sessions/day.
- **Polarization**: casual users leave in **under 2 minutes**; core users average **37 min/session** — a 15x gap. Most players dabble; a small core drives time and revenue.
- **Return**: **72% weekly return rate**; mobile = 78% of sessions among weekly returners.
- **Gen Z breadth**: Gen Z users tried an **average of 21 different experiences per month** in 2025 (playersforlife.com).

## Q7 — Development: team sizes, build times, solo dev achievements

### Typical teams & timelines
- **Norm for hits**: small teams. "Two people on Roblox can build a game that gets 6,000 concurrents or more — there's no need to build out massive teams… You shouldn't spend more than two to four weeks on a new concept — deploy it to 1,000 users for a few dollars and evolve from there" — Looty Games (`index`, https://www.PocketGamer.Biz/looty-games-on-monetising-roblox-when-you-start-developing-on-roblox-you-really-need-to-throw-away-a-lot-of-what-you-already-know-about-building-games/).
- **Build times**: simple games days–weeks; serious hits take months and usually **years of updates** before breaking out. "Many only succeed after years of regular updates and community feedback" (`index`, https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/teens-becoming-millionaires-making-games-in-roblox).
- **Blox Fruits**: started with Gamer Robot (tiny crew); community estimates now ~9 devs; monetization deliberately non-p2w-lootbox (`index`, https://naavik.co/digest/blox-fruits-lessons/; blox-fruits.fandom.com).
- **Brookhaven**: solo-built by Wolfpaq (2020–2025); post-acquisition Voldex staffs **15+ developers** on it (`index`, mmos.com).

### Economics of being a Roblox dev
- 9.5M+ developers on platform; 42,000+ in DevEx; ~38,000 qualified by Mar 2026.
- Earnings are superstar-distributed: 85% earn <$100/month; median DevEx ~$1,500/yr; top 1,000 avg $1.3M (`index`, techtimes.com).
- Common playbook: prototype in weeks, ship free, iterate with Discord/TikTok community, layer monetization after retention, run live-ops (events, seasons, "admin abuse" livestreams) rather than shipping sequels.
